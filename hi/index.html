While you are here

Open models, open weights, open data.

I spend a lot of my time in natural language processing and large language models, and lately most of it with Olmo 3, Ai2's fully open model family. Not just open weights: the training data, code, and checkpoints are all published, so you can see how the whole thing was built and build on it yourself.

My current project is a text embedding model for retrieval, fine-tuned from the Olmo 3 7B base with LoRA and a contrastive loss, released under Apache 2.0 on Hugging Face.

And come play

Move Together, Play Together.

That is the whole idea behind House Tandem, the indie studio I run in Columbus, Ohio. Our games are driven by motion, and the best controller is the one already in your pocket or in your hand. The gyro in your phone works, and so does the gyro in a DualSense, a Steam Controller, a GameSir, or a Switch Pro controller.

Shake to roll 3D dice. Flick your wrist to push coins across a carnival dozer. Grab a friend and balance a tandem bike together in Tandemonium, where one of you steers and the other pedals, and you only stay upright if you move together.

Most of them play right in the browser or as a Discord Activity, no download, no console. There is also an open source Controller Lab that shows gamepad and gyro input live in 3D for every controller we support, if you like poking at how motion input works.

Come play games with me. Mention it in your email and I will send you an invite. Move together, play together.

I look forward to talking with you about the depths of AI, NLP, ML, and our new world on the Internet.
